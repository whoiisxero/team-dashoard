2. Set up a private member

First invite or create the member’s email in Authentication → Users. Then fill in this form. The generated query sets their welcome name and business, removes team-project and other-chat access, and creates a private chat with the existing owner. Their notes, calendar and quotes stay private to their account.

View member setup SQL

Run the copied query as postgres in the SQL Editor. The result must show personal_only = true, shared_access = false and private_rooms = 1. The member can then sign in; complete My settings once to open their workspace. Existing notes and quotes are preserved.

3. Find events and notes

After refreshing Quest, the member can open the menu near Home:

  • Business workspace for services and client work; it uses the saved business name.
  • Quotes and Invoices for saved rand documents, email drafts and print/save PDF. Email drafts open in the member’s mail app; Quest does not confirm sending or automatically attach a PDF.
  • My calendar → Add event for private appointments, dates, times, locations and event notes. Click a date to plan that day.
  • Writing & notes → New note for writing, ideas and drafts.
  • Business & work → New business item for work notes, progress and deadlines.

These pages are private to the signed-in account. They do not grant access to team projects or the shared calendar. On a phone, tap the menu button to find them.

4. Personal reminders

A member can add a reminder to a personal event or a business item. Reminders appear in her private Notifications inbox while Quest is open. Phone push notifications and background delivery are planned for the next milestone.
