Quest / Owner setup

Cas’s private workspace.

Copy the complete setup query below, run it in Supabase to add the private calendar and notes features. Use Members to give Cas her personal-only workspace if you have not done that yet. This setup does not enable phone push notifications.

1. Update the private workspace features

  1. Click Copy complete setup SQL.
  2. Open the Quest Supabase SQL Editor. Create a new query and select the postgres role.
  3. Paste the complete query. Click at the end to clear any text selection, then Run.
  4. The result should say Quest workspace setup ready. Refresh Quest.

2. Give Cas her private space

If Cas already shows Personal workspace only in Members, skip this step. Otherwise, in Quest, open Members → Refresh. On Cas’s card, choose Create personal-only workspace and confirm. This removes her project and other-chat access, and creates a conversation shared only with you.

Ask Cas to sign out and sign in. Her first screen will let her choose her welcome name, colours, reading size, spacing and time zone. Her writing and business notes remain private to her account—even from the owner.

3. Find events and notes

After refreshing Quest, Cas can open the menu near Home:

  • My calendar → Add event for private appointments, dates, times, locations and event notes. Click a date to plan that day.
  • Writing & notes → New note for writing, ideas and drafts.
  • Business & work → New business item for work notes, progress and deadlines.

These pages are private to the signed-in account. They do not grant access to team projects or the shared calendar. On a phone, tap the menu button to find them.

4. Personal reminders

Cas can add a reminder to a personal event or a business item. Reminders appear in her private Notifications inbox while Quest is open. Phone push notifications and background delivery are planned for the next milestone.
